# 9-zip Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This policy covers 9-zip (listed in the Microsoft Store as "9-zip Archiver"), published by Vidyasagar Academy and developed by Prof. Dattaraj S. Vidyasagar and Prof. Yash D. Vidyasagar.

## What we collect

Nothing. 9-zip does not collect, store, share or transmit any personal information. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no telemetry, and it does not connect to the internet.

## Your files

9-zip reads and writes only the files and folders you choose when you create, open, test or extract archives. Those files stay on your computer.

9-zip keeps one small settings file on your computer, holding your favorite folders. It is removed when you uninstall the app.

## Links

The website and email links in the About window open in your own browser or email program.
